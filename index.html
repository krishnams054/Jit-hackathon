FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers to common questions about participation, eligibility, rules, and amenities.

What is the squad size limit for CLOCKIT 2026? +
Teams can comprise 2 to 4 members. Inter-college and cross-department squads are warmly welcomed to foster diverse innovation.
What is the registration fee to participate? +
The registration fee is ₹650 per squad (for the entire team of 2 to 4 adventurers). This includes full 24-hour non-stop meals, midnight fuel & coffee, high-speed Wi-Fi, rest pods, official certificates, and exclusive swag.
What amenities will be provided during the 24 hours? +
Participants will receive complimentary breakfast, lunch, high-tea, dinner, and midnight coffee/snacks. High-speed gigabit Wi-Fi, power backup, and designated rest/recharge pods will be active throughout.
Can I build on an existing project or GitHub repository? +
All code, commits, and assets submitted must be conceived and forged during the 24-hour sprint. Open-source libraries and public APIs are permitted, but preexisting complete codebases are strictly disqualified.
Who can I contact if I have further queries? +
